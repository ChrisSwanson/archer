# Why Archer?

Give your coding agent a picture you can both point at. Archer keeps Mermaid diagrams, Markdown notes and the feedback around them beside your code, ready for the next conversation.

## Understand the codebase

Start with people, systems and boundaries. Drill into containers and components where the question needs detail.

> Show me the big picture of this project, then the containers that make it work.

## Follow a path

Ask the agent to highlight an existing diagram. Select a part to give your follow-up context.

> Show me how a request reaches storage. Highlight the path on our existing diagram.

## Review a proposal

Put a proposed change beside the current design. Comment on a connection, revise the proposal and keep it labelled until you agree.

> Propose moving this job into a worker. Show the new boundary and what crosses it.

## Keep the understanding

Docs mode maintains a small architecture pack with an index for future agents. Saved walkthroughs let people replay an explanation step by step.

> Keep our architecture docs current. Answer from the diagrams we already have.

## Choose a view

| Question | View |
